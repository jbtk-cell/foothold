---
title: Repeating Yourself
summary: Loops - the thing computers are genuinely better at than people.
---

A person asked to add up a thousand numbers will make a mistake. A computer
will not, and it will not get bored, and this module is where you learn to ask
it.

Loops are also where beginners meet their first program that never stops. That
is fine. Foothold will notice and kill it for you.
