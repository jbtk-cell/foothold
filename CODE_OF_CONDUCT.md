# Code of Conduct

Foothold is for people who are learning, which means it is for people who will
ask questions with obvious answers and get things wrong in public. Protecting
that is the whole point of this document.

## Expected

- Assume the person asking has less context than you, not less intelligence.
- Answer the question that was asked before suggesting a better question.
- When you disagree, do it about the work.

## Not accepted

- Condescension towards beginners. "Did you even read the docs" is never the
  answer, and neither is any variation of it.
- Harassment, personal attacks, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Demeaning comments about someone's background, identity, or experience level.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else the
project is being represented.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening an issue, or privately to the repository owner
through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject contributions and comments that do not follow this document,
and may block accounts for repeated or severe behaviour.
